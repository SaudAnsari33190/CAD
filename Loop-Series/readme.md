CAD Loop Experiments — 5, 7, 9, 11 & 13

A small series of Fusion 360 geometry experiments where I progressively increased the number of loops from 5 → 7 → 9 → 11 → 13.

🧩 Backstory

This started as part of my attempt to get back into CAD after a break. I wanted to avoid simply following tutorials, so I began making increasingly weird geometric objects just to see how far I could push the modelling process.

The loop series became one of those experiments.

🛠️ Made With
Autodesk Fusion 360
Parametric modelling
Sketching & patterning
3D rendering
📈 Progress

5 → 7 → 9 → 11 → 13

Each version is a small step in understanding geometry, symmetry, repetition, and Fusion 360's workflow.

🚀 What's Next?

The goal is to move from pure geometry experiments → functional mechanical designs, eventually combining CAD with my interests in electronics, embedded systems, and robotics.

Learning by making — one weird CAD model at a time.
